## Biggest Weaknesses

1. **No mentoring, onboarding, or cross-team influence mentioned.** This is the #1 filter for Staff roles. You must show you made *other engineers better*.
2. **"Collaborated on ASP.NET Core"** — passive phrasing undermines your full-stack claim. Change to owned work or remove.
3. **Impact metrics are effort-based, not outcome-based.** "300+ tests" → "Cut regression defects by X%". "10,000+ markers" → "Maintained sub-60fps on low-end hardware with 10K concurrent markers".
4. **Projects section is unfocused.** 11 repos on README. Staff candidate shows 3-4 *meaningful* projects with architecture notes.
5. **M.Sc. thesis not connected to software.** Bridge the gap explicitly (systems thinking from mechatronics applied to frontend architecture).
